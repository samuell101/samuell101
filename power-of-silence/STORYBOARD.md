# THE POWER OF SILENCE — Production Storyboard

**Format:** 9:16 vertical · 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps · 45 s · premium cinematic 2D
**Language:** Roman Telugu–English VO · **Audience:** 18–30 · **Mood:** mysterious → powerful → inspiring
**Core rule:** every scene shows *why* his silence changes the outcome, not just that he stays silent.

Every scene follows the same four beats: **Situation → Natural reaction → Chooses silence → Visible benefit.**

---

## 0. Global production bible

### Palette (no other saturated colours)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#17191F` | base of every environment |
| Primary text | `#F2EDE3` | captions, eye whites, paper |
| Accent | `#D6A84B` | highlighted words, rim light, lamps, the “benefit” visuals |
| Secondary | `#252830` | walls, mid-ground architecture |
| Shadow | `#0C0D11` | vignettes, ground shadows, cut-to-black |

Supporting characters wear **muted** clothes only (desaturated maroon, olive, steel blue, beige, rust) so the gold always means “control / clarity.”

### Lighting
- One motivated key light per scene (window, street lamp, pendant, desk lamp), always warm gold.
- The protagonist gets a **gold rim on the light side** of every body part. This keeps the black outfit readable on charcoal.
- Ambient fill is cool slate. Every shot gets a soft radial vignette (near-black at the edges).
- Light gets **warmer and brighter the more control he shows**: cold in Scene 2's anger, warm in Scene 6.

### Protagonist — model sheet (locked, reused in every shot)
- Male, ~22, slim athletic build, natural proportions (≈ 7.3 heads tall).
- **Hair:** medium-length black, volume on top, fringe falling to the right across the forehead, sides to the tops of the ears. Same silhouette in every shot.
- **Outfit:** black zip jacket (open), dark slate crew-neck tee, black trousers, black shoes with grey sole.
- **Skin:** warm medium-brown `#B97D58`, shadow `#93603F`.
- **Face:** expressive brows (the main acting tool), almond eyes with dark-brown irises and a catch-light, small straight nose, neutral mouth.
- **Rig rules:** hips are the root. Torso leans around the hips. Arms connect at the shoulders, legs at the hips. No floating limbs. Elbows and knees bend only the natural way. Blinks every 2.6–4.4 s. Breathing scales the chest 3–4 %.

| ID | Scene | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **B** “the shouter” | 2 | curly brown hair, maroon tee, navy jeans |
| **F1** “the questioner” | 3 | side-part hair, steel-blue shirt |

### Typography
- **Main titles / scene captions:** Bebas Neue, tracked +5 to +14, ivory, with the key word in gold `#D6A84B` and a soft gold glow.
- **Supporting text** (handwriting in the notebook, UI): Poppins SemiBold / Medium.
- 2–5 words per line, centred, in the **upper safe area (y ≈ 290–520 px)**, above heads and never over a face.
- In: 0.45 s fade + 34 px slide-up (ease-out cubic). Out: 0.4 s fade.
- Captions are **hidden during close-ups** (fist, eyes) so the acting carries those moments.

### Camera language
Slow push-ins, tracking shots, match cuts, selective slow motion, depth parallax (background layers move at 0.7× camera). Shake only on the table slam (0.2 s, ≤ 4 px). No random zooms.

## SCENE 01 — THE HOOK · 0:00–0:05 (5 s)

**VO:** “Nee life lo konni situations lo nuvvu matladakunda undatame nee biggest strength avuthundi. Eppudo thelusa?”
**Caption:** `THE POWER OF` / **`SILENCE`** (gold) + thin gold rule — 0.4 s → 4.3 s

| | |
|---|---|
| **Composition** | Full-body wide. Night street plaza. Protagonist dead centre, two rows of crowd around him (back row smaller and higher, front row large at the frame edges). |
| **Background** | Slate building silhouettes with scattered warm windows, a street lamp on the right with a gold pool of light, cool-to-charcoal sky gradient, ground fading to near-black. |
| **Lighting** | Key from the street lamp (upper right). Gold rim on the protagonist's right edges. Crowd lit flatter and dimmer. |
| **Character positioning** | Protagonist x = centre, feet on the floor line. 8 crowd members: 2 walk across (one each way), 6 argue in pairs facing each other. |
| **Actions 0–2.6 s** | Crowd: overlapping gestures (hands up, pointing, shrugging), mouths moving fast, ivory “sound-wave” arcs coming out of the mouths, slight torso sway. Protagonist: still, head and eyes flicking left → right → left toward the noise, brows raised in confusion, a slow breath. |
| **2.6 s — FREEZE** | Every crowd member stops mid-gesture. A dark overlay (55 %) drops over the world, the sound-wave arcs vanish, and the protagonist is redrawn on top with a stronger rim, as if the world paused and only he is awake. |
| **2.8–3.6 s** | He slowly turns his head to camera (look −0.6 → 0) and his eyes settle on the lens. |
| **Expression** | Confusion (worried brows) → calm (level brows, a slight narrowing of the eyes, a faint confident smirk). |
| **Camera** | 0–3 s: almost-static drift up (6 px/s), zoom 1.0 → 1.06. 3.0–5.0 s: accelerating push-in toward his left eye, zoom 1 → ×46. |
| **Animation notes** | Hair has a subtle sheen. Blinks stop once he looks at camera, so the stare reads as intentional. Breathing continues. |
| **Sound** | Crowd chatter → **hard audio drop** at 2.6 s with a low soft impact; the bed returns slowly from 3.2 s. |
| **Transition** | The push-in ends inside his pupil (the frame goes to warm black at 4.55–5.0 s). **Match cut:** Scene 2 opens as an iris reveal out of that same pupil. |

## SCENE 02 — WHEN YOU'RE ANGRY · 0:05–0:13 (8 s)

**VO:** “Kopam lo unnappudu silent ga undu. Endukante aa moment lo nuvvu gelichina argument, nee relationship ni odipoyela cheyochu.”
**Caption:** `CONTROL YOUR` / **`ANGER`** (gold) — 6.0 s → 12.4 s, hidden during the fist close-up

| | |
|---|---|
| **Composition** | Medium-wide two-shot (zoom 1.42). **B** on the left facing right, **protagonist** on the right facing left, 440 px apart. |
| **Background** | Panelled interior wall. A **gold-lit window on the left** casting a diagonal light shaft across the floor. Dark doorway far right (where he will exit). |
| **Lighting** | Key from the window (left). Rim on the left edges of both characters. Cooler and harsher than the other scenes. |
| **0–0.7 s** | Iris opens from the pupil. |
| **0.7–3.4 s — situation & reaction** | B jabs a pointing finger (arm horizontal, jab oscillating ~1.4 Hz), other hand on hip, leaning in, mouth shouting, brows angry. The protagonist's anger builds: brows pull down and together (0 → 100 %), eyes narrow, mouth presses into a frown, **right fist slowly clenches** (2.0–2.8 s), torso leans forward, and he takes one step toward B (3.0–3.4 s). |
| **3.3–5.0 s — slow-down** | Camera pushes in to a **close-up of the clenched fist** (zoom 4). The fist trembles slightly. A heartbeat pulses the vignette (≈ 70 bpm) and the frame darkens 18 %. |
| **4.4–5.8 s — the choice** | He takes a **deep breath** (chest expands, eyes close softly), the **fist opens** (5.0–5.4 s), his brows relax, and the camera pulls back to medium. |
| **5.6–8.0 s — benefit** | He steps back, turns away (side −0.7 → +0.75), and walks right with a full walk cycle (arm swing, heel-toe, hip bob). B is left standing: his arm drops, his anger turns into puzzlement (worried brows, mouth closed), and his eyes follow the protagonist. |
| **Visual message** | The argument is never “won.” He protects the relationship and his dignity, and the person who shouted is the one left looking small. |
| **Camera** | Medium → fist CU push-in (slow motion feel) → pull back → **side tracking shot** following his walk (camera x 560 → 1000). |
| **Sound** | Subtle high tension tone, then a cloth creak on the fist clench, heartbeat (lub-dub), a long exhale, footsteps. |
| **Transition** | **Natural wipe:** he walks past the lens. A giant, softly blurred foreground version of him (black jacket with a gold rim edge) sweeps left → right and fills the frame at 13.0 s. Scene 3 is revealed behind it. |

## SCENE 04 — WHEN SOMEONE PROVOKES YOU · 0:21–0:29 (8 s)

**VO:** “Prathi provocation ki response ivvaku. Nuvvu react ayithe, nee emotions ni control chese power vallaki ichinatte.”
**Caption:** `DON'T REACT.` / **`STAY IN CONTROL`** (gold) — 26.6 s → 28.9 s

| | |
|---|---|
| **Composition** | Hallway with lockers. Wide (zoom 1.3): **D** far left, **C** left by a small table, **protagonist** right with his back half-turned to them. |
| **Background** | Repeating slate lockers with handles, overhead strip lights with gold pools of light, near-black floor. |
| **Lighting** | Top-down key from the strip lights; rim on the heads and shoulders. |
| **0–0.85 s — slam** | Close-up (zoom 3) on the table: C's hand slams down (fast ease-in), impact lines, the cup jumps, 0.2 s micro-shake. |
| **0.85–1.5 s** | Pull back to the wide shot. |
| **1.0–2.8 s — provocation** | C and D point and laugh: heads tipping back, mouths wide, bodies shaking, one hand on the stomach. |
| **2.6–3.3 s** | C walks over and **shoves the protagonist's shoulder**. He sways forward 18 px and leans. C steps back, grinning. |
| **3.35–3.9 s — natural reaction** | He turns toward them. His brows tighten (anger 55 %). |
| **3.9–5.5 s — close-up of the eyes** | Zoom 6 on his eyes. **A pause, and music drops to silence.** His eyes lock on them, then the anger leaves his brows, his eyelids soften, and he blinks once slowly with a long exhale. Small, controlled smile. |
| **5.5–6.7 s — the choice** | **Slow-motion turn away** (1.0 s). The camera pulls back. |
| **6.6–8.0 s — benefit** | He walks right, calm and even-paced, while the camera **tracks with him**. C and D are left behind: their laughter dies and their arms drop. They look confused (worried brows) because the reaction they wanted never came. |
| **Visual message** | You can't control what they do. You control your reaction, and by not reacting he keeps the power. |
| **Sound** | Slam impact, laughter bursts, shove, **0.7 s of near-total silence** in the eye close-up, then footsteps. |
| **Transition** | The camera keeps following him: warm gold light floods in from the right (where he is heading) and dissolves into the meeting room of Scene 5, which he enters from the left. |

## Final QC checklist
1. ✅ One protagonist rig (same hair, outfit, skin, proportions) reused in every shot, including the back view and the wipe.
2. ✅ Limbs are rig-connected (hip/shoulder roots, fixed bone lengths). No floating parts.
3. ✅ Each scene plays Situation → Reaction → Silence → **visible benefit** (B left alone; work done by dawn; mockers deflated; insight written down; distractions dissolve).
4. ✅ Every transition is motivated: eye push-in → pupil iris, walk-past wipe, notebook→slam match cut, follow-through into the next room, notebook close → walk into light.
5. ✅ Captions spelled exactly, 2–5 words per line, upper safe area, hidden on close-ups.
6. ✅ Palette limited to the five tokens plus muted wardrobe; no neon.
7. ✅ Ending: clean dark card with the final phrase bold, centred, gold.
